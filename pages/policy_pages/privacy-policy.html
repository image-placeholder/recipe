Transparency & Trust

Privacy Policy
Your Data & The Archive

This policy explains how Crediton Table handles information. Our primary goal is the preservation of community heritage, not the collection of personal data.

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information Collection

Crediton Table is designed as a static digital archive. We do not require users to create accounts, subscribe to newsletters, or provide personal details to access the recipes.

  • Log Data: Like most websites, our hosting provider may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, browser types) to ensure site security and performance.
  • Cookies: We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any cookies used are strictly for essential site functionality.

2. External Links & Donations

Our site contains links to external platforms, including creditonuc.org and third-party payment processors for donations.

When you click a link to donate, you are leaving Crediton Table. We do not see or store your credit card information or billing details. These transactions are governed by the privacy policies of the respective payment providers and the Church.

3. Community Contributions

If you contact us via email or GitHub to provide feedback, suggest a recipe, or report a typo, we will only use your contact information to respond to your inquiry. We will never sell or distribute your email address to third parties.

4. Open Source Transparency

As an open-source project, our code is public. While this promotes transparency, please be aware that any information you contribute to our public GitHub repository (such as comments on "Issues" or "Pull Requests") becomes part of the public record.

Contact

For questions regarding this policy or the handling of community heritage data, please reach out via our GitHub repository or the contact methods provided in the site footer.
