Keep the Heritage Alive

Support Crediton Table
Preserve, Share, Remember

Your support helps maintain this digital archive of Crediton Church community recipes. Every contribution keeps the site online, funds hosting costs, allows for improvements, and ensures that the flavors and stories of South Huron remain accessible for generations.

How to Donate

Donations can be made directly to the site owner to support hosting, domain costs, and future improvements. Any surplus funds will, in good faith, be allocated either to the church or towards other heritage-related projects.

Note: The site owner is not affiliated with Crediton Church. Donations made here are not eligible for tax receipts. If you prefer, donations can also be made directly to the church:

  • Zion United Church Crediton — check creditonuc.org for e-transfer or direct giving instructions.

This ensures your gift goes directly to the church while supporting community heritage.

Other Ways to Contribute

  • Share the archive: Spread the word with friends, family, or social media — every visitor keeps the story alive.
  • Give feedback: Found a typo, outdated info, or have a story to share? Your input improves the archive for everyone.
  • Contribute similar projects: If you maintain or know of other heritage projects, collaborating helps preserve community traditions.
  • Open source contributions: The website’s source code is available on GitHub. Developers can suggest improvements, fixes, or enhancements.
  • International support: Even from outside Canada, you can help by donating to the church directly online, sharing the archive, or contributing digital skills.

Disclaimer

Crediton Table is a private initiative by the site owner and is not affiliated with the Crediton Church. Donations made to the website are not tax-deductible. Any funds contributed are used at the discretion of the site owner for website maintenance, improvements, or in good faith to support church or heritage projects. No guarantees are made regarding fund allocation.
