Raised in the Crediton community, Jared grew up surrounded by the recipes, traditions, and shared meals of the Crediton Church. He founded {{site.title}} to modernize, digitize, and protect these cherished cookbooks for generations, ensuring the food stories of South Huron are never lost.

About Jared

Jared was raised in Crediton, Ontario, and has always been deeply connected to the community’s culinary traditions. Inspired by the church cookbooks he grew up with, he sought to modernize and digitize the recipes while preserving their original charm.

His mission is not just to archive recipes, but to celebrate the stories, gatherings, and flavors that brought families together over decades. Through {{site.title}}, Jared ensures that these recipes remain accessible, shareable, and meaningful for future generations.

Jared’s Journey

Early Life

Growing up in Crediton

Raised in the heart of the Crediton Church community, Jared developed a love for local traditions and family recipes from an early age.

Discovery

Unearthing the Cookbooks

Jared collected decades of church cookbooks from community members, recognizing their value in preserving South Huron’s culinary heritage.

Digitization

Modernizing the Recipes

Each recipe was carefully digitized, normalized, and annotated to ensure future generations can cook, share, and enjoy them without losing their original charm.

Launch

Sharing {{site.title}}

Jared launched {{site.title}}, creating a digital archive that celebrates the community’s recipes and stories, preserving them for generations to come.
