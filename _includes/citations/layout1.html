Reference & Attribution

How to Cite This Project

"Crediton Table is a community preservation project. Citation helps keep the people and traditions behind it visible."

Chicago Style

Van Valkengoed, Jared. Crediton Table: A Living Archive. Community digitization project. South Huron, Ontario. Accessed [date].

MLA Style

Van Valkengoed, Jared. Crediton Table: A Living Archive. South Huron, Ontario. Accessed [date].

APA Style

Van Valkengoed, J. (n.d.). Crediton Table: A living archive. South Huron, Ontario.

Individual Recipes

“Recipe Name.” Crediton Table. Adapted from Crediton Church community cookbooks.

Acknowledge the community origin when possible.

A Note on Stewardship

These recipes were shared freely within a community context. Citation is a gesture of respect for the generations of contributors whose knowledge shaped this archive.
