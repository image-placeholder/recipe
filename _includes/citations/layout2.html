Reference & Attribution

How to Cite This Project

Crediton Table is a community preservation project. If you reference, quote, or build upon this work, please acknowledge its source so the people and traditions behind it remain visible.

General Citation

Crediton Table. A Living Archive of Crediton Church Community Recipes. Created by Jared Van Valkengoed. South Huron, Ontario. Accessed [date].

Academic or Research Use

Van Valkengoed, Jared. Crediton Table: A Living Archive. Community digitization project based on Crediton Church cookbooks (1950s–1980s). South Huron, Ontario.

Individual Recipes

Recipe name. Crediton Table. Adapted from Crediton Church community cookbooks, original contributors unknown.

When possible, acknowledge the community origin rather than claiming authorship.

A Note on Stewardship

These recipes were shared freely within a community context. Citation is not required by law, but it is encouraged as a gesture of respect for the generations of contributors whose knowledge shaped this archive.
